1. THE PROBLEM
================================================================================

Investing in Israeli real estate — especially in cities undergoing urban renewal
(Pinui-Binui / TAMA 38) — requires cross-referencing dozens of fragmented
government data sources: population statistics, housing construction pipelines,
municipal budgets, transport accessibility, environmental data, and more.

This information is scattered across multiple ministries on data.gov.il, each
with different field names (mostly in Hebrew), inconsistent city identifiers, and
no unified view. A prospective investor or researcher must manually download
CSV files, normalize city names, and build their own comparison framework.

HomeAI solves this by:
  - Fetching 10 government datasets automatically via the CKAN API
  - Normalizing Hebrew field names and city identifiers
  - Joining all data into a unified CityProfile for every city (pop > 5,000)
  - Computing a transparent, weighted Investment Score (0-100) for each city
  - Presenting everything in an interactive, bilingual (English + Hebrew RTL)
    web application with maps, charts, tables, and comparison tools


2. THE DATA
================================================================================

All data comes from Israel's open government data portal (data.gov.il) via the
CKAN API. The app fetches ~100,000+ records across these 10 datasets:

  Dataset                    Source Ministry                  ~Records
  -------------------------  -------------------------------- --------
  Urban Renewal Projects     Ministry of Housing                  752
  Construction Sites         Ministry of Housing               10,440
  Housing Inventory          Ministry of Housing                1,112
  Mechir LaMishtaken         Ministry of Housing                2,352
  Population (by age group)  Central Bureau of Statistics        1,284
  Bank Branches              Bank of Israel                     1,396
  Green Buildings            Min. of Environmental Protection    7,269
  Bus Stops                  Ministry of Transport              33,927
  Contaminated Sites         Min. of Environmental Protection      491
  Municipal Finances         Ministry of Interior               ~5,000

Data is cached in memory (30-min TTL) and on disk for resilience when the
government API is unavailable.


3. THE INVESTMENT SCORE
================================================================================

Each city receives a composite score from 0 to 100, built from 6 weighted
sub-scores. Most metrics are normalized per-capita (per 1,000 residents) and
then percentile-ranked against all other cities.

  Sub-Score               Weight   What It Measures
  ----------------------  ------   ----------------------------------------
  Development Momentum     25%     Urban renewal projects, additional housing
                                   units, construction sites, housing pipeline
                                   (all per capita)

  Demand Signal            20%     Subscriber-to-winner ratio in housing
                                   lotteries (demand proxy), young adult
                                   population share (19-45), age growth ratio

  Price Attractiveness     20%     Average price per square meter (inverted:
                                   lower price = higher score)

  Infrastructure Quality   15%     Bus stop density, bank branch density,
                                   green building count (all per capita)

  Municipal Health         10%     Budget surplus ratio, loan burden (inverted),
                                   income per capita

  Environment              10%     Contaminated sites per capita (inverted:
                                   fewer = better)

Score categories:
  - 70-100  Excellent (green)
  - 55-69   Good (blue)
  - 40-54   Fair (amber)
  - 0-39    Low (red)

If a city has fewer than 3 available sub-scores, a confidence penalty is
applied to prevent inflated scores from sparse data.


4. APP SCREENS — DETAILED BREAKDOWN
================================================================================


4.1  DASHBOARD (Home Page)
--------------------------
The landing page provides a national overview of the Israeli housing market.

Summary Cards (top row):
  - Total cities analyzed (count)
  - Average investment score across all cities
  - Cities with active urban renewal projects
  - Cities with price data available

Charts:
  - Two dynamically-loaded market summary charts

Top Cities Table:
  - Ranked list of top 50 cities by investment score
  - Columns: Rank, City (clickable link to City View), District, Investment
    Score (color-coded badge), Population, Avg Price (per sqm in shekels),
    Urban Renewal Projects (mini bar chart), Construction Sites (mini bar)
  - Interactive: sortable columns (click headers), search by city name,
    filter by district
  - Mini bar charts show relative scale against the maximum value


4.2  MAP / EXPLORE
------------------
An interactive map of Israel with color-coded city markers.

Map Layers (switchable):
  - "Score"        — color intensity by investment score
  - "Renewal"      — marker size by number of urban renewal projects
  - "Construction" — marker size by construction site count
  - "Price"        — color by average price per square meter

Filter Controls:
  - Search by city name
  - District dropdown
  - Minimum score threshold: 0, 45, 60, or 75

Quick Stats (4 cards, update with filters):
  - Cities shown on map
  - Average score of visible cities
  - Total renewal projects in view
  - Total construction sites in view

Top Opportunities:
  - 3x3 grid of the top 9 cities matching current filters
  - Each card shows city name, district, and score badge
  - Clickable — navigates to City View


4.3  CITY VIEW
--------------
The deepest view — a full profile for a single city. Accessed by clicking a
city name anywhere in the app, or by selecting from the search list.

City Selector:
  - Collapsible search panel with all cities listed
  - Shows rank, name, district, and score badge
  - Quick-pick buttons for the top 5 cities when nothing is selected

City Header:
  - City name (large), district, population, investment score (large badge)
  - Sticky navigation tabs: "Overview" and "Neighborhood Detail"

OVERVIEW TAB:

  City Map Card:
    - Leaflet map centered on the city
    - Geo-located markers for: green buildings, bus stops, bank branches,
      construction sites, urban renewal projects
    - Each data type has its own icon and color
    - Shows total geo-located data point count

  Score Breakdown:
    - Radar/spider chart of all 6 sub-scores
    - Detailed list: each sub-score with its weight, progress bar, and value
    - Tooltips explain what each dimension measures

  Urban Development Cards (4):
    - Urban Renewal Projects (total + in-execution count)
    - Additional Housing Units (total + ratio to existing units)
    - Construction Sites (total + active cranes count)
    - Housing Pipeline (potential units from inventory)

  Pricing & Demand Cards (4):
    - Average Price Per Meter (in shekels, or N/A)
    - Subscriber-to-Winner Ratio (demand proxy, or N/A)
    - Bus Stops (transit accessibility count)
    - Bank Branches (financial infrastructure count)

  Green & Demographics Cards (4):
    - Green Buildings (count + average green score)
    - Contaminated Sites (count + remediated count if any)
    - Young Adult Ratio (19-45 age group as percentage)
    - Population (total with district label)

  Municipal Finances Cards (4):
    - Budget Balance (surplus in thousands of shekels)
    - Annual Income (regular budget, in thousands)
    - Annual Expenses (regular budget, in thousands)
    - Loan Burden (outstanding loans, in thousands)

  Age Distribution Chart:
    - 6 bars for age groups: 0-5, 6-18, 19-45, 46-55, 56-64, 65+
    - Each bar shows its percentage of total population

NEIGHBORHOOD DETAIL TAB:

  Neighborhood Pricing:
    - Pricing breakdown by neighborhood/area within the city

  City Projects List:
    - Urban renewal projects (individual project details)
    - Construction sites (addresses, types, cranes)
    - Housing plans (plan numbers, stages)
    - Contaminated sites (contamination level, source)


4.4  PROJECTS
-------------
A comprehensive table of all ~769 individual urban renewal projects across
Israel, enriched with investment signals from their parent city.

Filter Controls:
  - Search by city name or project/complex name (Hebrew-aware)
  - District dropdown filter
  - Status filter: All / In Execution / Planned Only

Mini Charts (4 cards, update with filters):
  - Status Distribution — pie chart (in execution vs planned)
  - District Distribution — bar chart (projects per district, color-coded
    by average score)
  - Total Additional Units — aggregate sum
  - Average Investment Score — across filtered projects

Projects Table:
  - Columns: Project/Complex Name, City (linked to City View), Score (badge),
    Existing Units, Additional Units (+X format), Growth Ratio (color-coded:
    green >2x, amber 1-2x, red <1x), Track, Status (badge)
  - All columns are sortable (click headers, toggle asc/desc)
  - Some columns hidden on mobile for readability

Expandable Detail Panel (click any row):
  When a row is clicked, a full detail panel expands below it showing:
  - Project name, city, and district
  - Investment score, execution status, regulatory track
  - Detailed status text
  - Existing, additional, and proposed unit counts
  - Unit growth ratio
  - Total permits issued
  - Project number and plan number
  - Authorization date and validity year
  - City population
  - Clickable external links:
    * "View on Map" — opens the project location on a government map
    * "View Plan Documents" — opens official planning documents
  Both links open in a new browser tab.


4.5  COMPARE
------------
Side-by-side comparison of any two cities across 30+ metrics.

City Selection:
  - Two searchable city picker panels (City A and City B)
  - Quick-pick buttons suggest interesting comparison pairs

Comparison Sections (once both cities selected):

  Header: Both city names, districts, and large score badges

  Score Breakdown:
    - Overall score comparison
    - All 6 sub-scores with weights
    - Winning value highlighted in green
    - Desktop: 3-column layout (City A | Metric | City B)
    - Mobile: stacked layout

  Development:
    - Urban renewal projects, in-execution count
    - Additional housing units
    - Construction sites, active cranes
    - Housing pipeline units

  Pricing & Demand:
    - Average price per meter (lower highlighted as better)
    - Subscriber-to-winner ratio
    - Mechir LaMishtaken project count

  Infrastructure & Demographics:
    - Population
    - Young adult percentage (19-45)
    - Bus stops, bank branches
    - Green buildings
    - Contaminated sites (lower highlighted as better)

  Municipal Finances:
    - Budget balance, annual income, annual expenses
    - Loan burden (lower highlighted as better)


4.6  METHODOLOGY
----------------
Transparent documentation of how the investment score is calculated.

  Investment Score Explanation:
    - Description of the overall scoring approach
    - Color-coded category badges (Excellent / Good / Fair / Low)

  Sub-Score Cards (6 cards):
    - Each card shows the dimension name, weight, description, and the
      specific metrics used in its calculation

  Data Sources:
    - All 10 government datasets listed with:
      * Emoji icon matching the map layer icons
      * Dataset name
      * Record count
    - Icons: Urban Renewal, Construction, Housing, Mechir, Population,
      Bank Branches, Green Buildings, Bus Stops, Contaminated, Municipal

  Limitations:
    - Disclaimers about data availability, update frequency, and price
      data coverage


5. TECHNICAL STACK
================================================================================

  - Next.js 16 (App Router) with Turbopack
  - TypeScript
  - Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui components
  - next-intl for bilingual support (English + Hebrew RTL)
  - Recharts for charts (lazy-loaded, ~200KB bundle)
  - Leaflet for maps (SSR-disabled)
  - Firebase App Hosting for deployment
  - ISR (Incremental Static Regeneration) with 5-minute revalidation
  - In-memory + disk cache for government API data (30-min TTL)


6. HOW TO RUN LOCALLY
================================================================================

  cd home-investment-app
  npm install
  npm run dev

  Open http://localhost:3000

  No API keys needed — all data comes from the public data.gov.il CKAN API.
